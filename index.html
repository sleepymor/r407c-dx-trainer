R407C is a zeotropic blend: superheat uses the dew point, subcooling uses the bubble point. The pressure model is approximate (about ±5%), so confirm with your manufacturer's PT chart.

ODPS: Kriwan DELTA-P II 22 S 635

Nameplate: supply 115/230 V AC 50/60 Hz, ambient -30 to +70 °C, relay 240 V AC max 2.5 A. It screws into the Dorin oil pump housing and opens its relay if oil differential pressure stays below the cut-out for the whole delay time. Defaults: 0.8 bar cut-out and 90 s delay, as set by your team. Cut-in is assumed to be cut-out + 0.32 bar. A shorter delay makes training faster.
